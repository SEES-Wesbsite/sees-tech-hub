# Core Objective

The primary purpose of the SEES Tech Hub is to empower students to build undeniable tech careers. 

Every feature (S-Ranks, points, bounties, leaderboards, feeds) is merely a gamified mechanism to support this ultimate goal. 

## The Pillars of Value
1. **Mastering DSA & Leetcode**: Preparing students to pass technical interviews through competitive sprints and peer programming.
2. **Winning Hackathons**: Encouraging the building of real-world products and dominating university-level competitions to build strong portfolios.
3. **Big Tech Pipelines**: Facilitating direct knowledge transfer and mentorship from UNILAG alumni thriving at global companies like Bloomberg, Meta, Google, and Bank of Scotland.

*Guiding Principle*: When adding a new feature or designing a page, ask: "Does this directly help a student land a job, win a hackathon, or pass a technical interview?" If not, it is secondary.
